Session 6: AI Safety & Risk Management

Practical approaches to identifying and mitigating AI risks in business contexts

The Four Pillars of AI Risk

🛡️ Security Risks

Data breaches & unauthorized access
Prompt injection attacks
Model theft or reverse engineering

⚖️ Compliance Risks

GDPR, CCPA privacy violations
Industry-specific regulations
Audit trail requirements

🎯 Operational Risks

AI hallucinations & misinformation
Over-reliance on AI decisions
Model drift & performance decay

👥 Ethical Risks

Bias in hiring or lending
Lack of transparency
Job displacement concerns

Risk Scenario Assessment

Identify the primary risk category and mitigation strategy for each scenario:

Scenario 1: AI Resume Screening
Your HR team wants to use AI to automatically screen resumes and rank candidates.
Primary Risk: Ethical (Bias)
Mitigation: Regular bias audits, diverse training data, human oversight for final decisions, transparency in criteria
Scenario 2: Customer Data Analysis
Marketing wants to use AI to analyze customer purchase history and personal data for targeting.
Primary Risk: Compliance (Privacy)
Mitigation: Data minimization, consent management, anonymization, regular compliance audits
Scenario 3: Financial Decision AI
Finance team wants AI to automatically approve/deny loans or credit applications.
Primary Risk: Operational (Hallucination/Error)
Mitigation: Human-in-the-loop for high-value decisions, confidence thresholds, regular model validation
