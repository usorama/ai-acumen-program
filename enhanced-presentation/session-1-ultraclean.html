The Three AI Ecosystems

We're not here to get caught up in AI hype. We're here to make strategic decisions.

OpenAI

"The Reasoning Engine"

Advanced problem-solving with o-series models. Ideal for complex reasoning and creative generation.

Google

"The Integrated Workflow"

Seamless productivity integration with Workspace. Perfect for business process automation.

Anthropic

"The Trusted Partner"

Safety & reliability focus with Claude. Best for legal/financial analysis.

Platform choice should be driven by specific business needs, not marketing claims.

Core Philosophies

OpenAI: Advanced reasoning, creative generation, agentic capabilities
→ Complex problem-solving, research, code generation
Google: Workspace integration, multimodal capabilities, enterprise agents
→ Business process automation, collaborative content
Anthropic: Large context window, low hallucination, safety focus
→ Legal/financial analysis, compliance monitoring
