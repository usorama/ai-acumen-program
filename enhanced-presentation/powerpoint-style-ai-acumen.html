The Three AI Ecosystems

Understanding platform strengths and strategic positioning

OpenAI Ecosystem

Strengths: First-mover advantage, extensive plugin ecosystem, strong developer community

Best For: Content creation, code generation, general business applications

Pricing: Usage-based, scales with volume

Google AI Platform

Strengths: Enterprise integration, search capabilities, robust infrastructure

Best For: Data analysis, enterprise workflows, search-enhanced applications

Pricing: Enterprise-focused, volume discounts

Anthropic Claude

Strengths: Safety-first design, constitutional AI, excellent reasoning capabilities

Best For: Strategic analysis, complex reasoning, high-stakes decisions

Pricing: Premium positioning, quality-focused

Strategic Platform Selection Guide

Key decision criteria for enterprise AI adoption

Technical Considerations

  • API reliability and uptime requirements
  • Data privacy and security protocols
  • Integration complexity with existing systems
  • Scalability and performance needs

Business Considerations

  • Total cost of ownership (TCO) analysis

  • Vendor lock-in and migration flexibility

AI ROI Calculation Framework

Systematic approach to measuring AI business value

ROI = (Benefits - Costs) / Costs × 100

Quantifiable Benefits

  • Time savings (hours per task × hourly rate)
  • Error reduction (cost of errors × frequency)
  • Increased output (additional units × unit value)
  • Customer satisfaction improvements

Total Costs

  • Platform subscription costs
  • Implementation and training time
  • Infrastructure and integration costs
  • Ongoing maintenance and optimization

The PTCF Framework

Systematic approach to professional AI prompting

Purpose

Define the 'why': What business objective are you trying to achieve?

Example: "I need to create a customer retention strategy to reduce churn by 15%"

Task

Specify the 'what': What specific action should the AI perform?

Example: "Analyze customer data and recommend 5 specific retention tactics"

Context

Provide the 'background': What information does the AI need to know?

Example: "Our SaaS company has 5,000 customers, avg $50/month, 8% monthly churn"

Format

Structure the 'how': How should the output be presented?

Example: "Provide recommendations in executive summary format with implementation timeline"

PTCF in Action

Before and after transformation example

❌ Poor Prompt

"Help me write a marketing email"

Issues: Vague, no context, no specific outcome defined

✅ PTCF-Optimized Prompt

"PURPOSE: Increase webinar attendance for our Q1 product launch
TASK: Write a compelling email sequence (3 emails over 2 weeks)
CONTEXT: B2B SaaS audience, 15,000 subscribers, 22% open rate, new AI features
FORMAT: Subject lines + body copy, personalization tokens included"
