### AI Tools for Market Research

**OpenAI GPT-4 for Market Analysis**
- **Strengths**: Complex research synthesis, trend analysis, strategic insights
- **Use Cases**: Industry reports, competitive analysis, market opportunity assessment
- **Example**: "Analyze the impact of remote work trends on commercial real estate markets in major US cities"

**Google Gemini for Market Intelligence**
- **Strengths**: Real-time data integration, multi-source analysis, collaborative reports
- **Use Cases**: Market sizing, customer segment analysis, competitive monitoring
- **Example**: Search trends analysis combined with demographic data for product positioning

**Anthropic Claude for Market Research**
- **Strengths**: Large document analysis, accurate data interpretation, compliance-aware insights
